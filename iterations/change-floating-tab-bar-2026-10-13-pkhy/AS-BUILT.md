# change-floating-tab-bar-2026-10-13-pkhy — as built

What was actually built, ledger row by ledger row, against the approved plan in
[`KICKOFF.md`](KICKOFF.md). Started 2026-10-13.

Verified in the real `SectionBarShell` and `index.css`, served by the Vite dev server in a scratch
page (a memory router, every room filled with rows to scroll under the glass, the shipped locales
loaded) and driven by Playwright Chromium at 390 × 844, 375 × 667 and 820 × 1180. clinozy-be was not
reachable in this container (Docker down), so the signed-in app itself was not opened; the scratch
page was deleted before the commit. The safe-area inset was emulated by serving `index.css` with
`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` replaced by `34px`.

Commits: `98ac7b4` (the bar, first as utilities), `82453d3` (its material moved into `index.css`
within the CSS budget — D14), and the close.

## Summary

- **Planned, not built** (deferred, user OK): — every ledger row is done.
- **Planned, not kept:** the near-opaque fallback where blur is unsupported (D15 — no supported
  browser lacks it).
- **Built, not planned:** the dark-mode glass (D12); the inset-outline hairline (D11); the CSS
  budget's declared exemption, 35.9 → 36.3 kB (D14).
- **Follow-ups for the next iteration:**

    under the tab bar — `<main>`'s padding cannot reach past content that overflows the `min-h-0`
    frame. Pre-existing since the launch (D13); a fix has to keep the inner-scroller rooms working.
  - Re-capture the ten phone baselines of `tests/e2e/visual.e2e.test.ts` (`room-*-phone-*.png`):
    they draw the old bar. The suite needs clinozy-be on `:3000`, which this container could not
    run.
  - The capsule in the signed-in app on a real iPhone (Safari's `backdrop-filter`, the real
    home-indicator inset) — measured here in Chromium with the inset emulated.
  - Lower the CSS budget again as soon as anything in the first paint can leave it (D14).
