# Drawer and section audit — feat-payment-celebration-2026-10-15-xpkw

Every drawer, sheet and section measured against the app's motion system
(`docs/reference/design.md` §5; `change-motion-consistency`), in the review format: one row per
finding. A survey of every overlay and route island on 2026-10-15.

## Findings

| Before                                                                                                                                                         | After                                                                                           | Why                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bottom sheets: vaul's own `slideFromBottom`, 500ms, on All sections (phone) and a phone confirm; the picker sheet alone overrides it (260ms rise, no exit)     | Every bottom sheet rises 28px and fades in 260ms on the sheet curve, no exit — in the primitive | One sheet, one motion; 500ms is past the 300ms ceiling                              |
| All sections' `animate-sheet-up` loses to vaul's unlayered rule (no `!`)                                                                                       | The primitive's `!` override, so no caller needs one                                            | The class did nothing                                                               |
| A move between a room's tabs (Medical, Stock, Payments, Offerings, Users) replays the page rise over the unchanged header; the track remounts, its thumb jumps | Only what sits under the toolbar rises; the thumb travels across the remount                    | Animate what changed; the same gesture moves the same way in every room             |
| A move between the patient file's sections replays the rise over the unchanged file bar, header and rail                                                       | Only the section rises                                                                          | As above                                                                            |
| The feedback carousel and "reveal in the list" scroll smoothly under reduced motion                                                                            | They jump                                                                                       | A scripted smooth scroll ignores the global reduce rule                             |
| Custom toggles (the role switch card, drawer choice controls): thumb 150ms on the default curve                                                                | 200ms on the strong ease-in-out, like the shared switch                                         | One switch                                                                          |
| Task rules popover retimes the shared switch (`duration-150`)                                                                                                  | No caller retimes it                                                                            | As above                                                                            |
| The setup steps drawer's progress bar on `ease-in-out` 200ms; the setup guide's on the primitive's                                                             | Both on the primitive's                                                                         | One progress bar                                                                    |
| The cookie banner: 300ms on `ease`                                                                                                                             | 240ms on the strong ease-out                                                                    | Entrances on the strong ease-out, under 300ms                                       |
| The payment drawers end in a toast                                                                                                                             | The Payment received view (the celebration, then the review ask)                                | A rare, meaningful moment earns its motion (frequency table: occasional → standard) |
