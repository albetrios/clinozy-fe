## Summary

- **Planned, not built:** —
- **Built, not planned:** the compound `.rx-segment.rx-days-other` selector (#1).
- **Follow-ups:** the 36 kB budget has ≈ 10 B of headroom on `main`. A screen that opens on demand
  should keep its own sizes in a stylesheet that loads with it, as the drawer now does.

## Standing deviations

Against the seven iterations it corrects: the allergy band, the row warning, the Pad line and the
Rx check card take the theme's `destructive-tint` / `destructive-border` instead of their 5–8 %
and 20–40 % mixes. The emphasised warning's ring is 2px, not 3px, and its text keeps at least
180px, not 192px. The Rx check column's padding is 16px, not 20px. The Pad's key hint is the
drawer footer's. Every size on a prescription line is unchanged.
